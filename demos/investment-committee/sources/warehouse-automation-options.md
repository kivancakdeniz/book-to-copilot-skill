# Northstar Warehouse Automation Options

**Document ID:** NWA-7  
**Status:** Fictional and synthetic  
**Currency:** EUR, values precomputed for the demo

## Objectives

1. Increase peak throughput by at least 25% before the 2027 holiday period.
2. Improve order accuracy to at least 99.5%.
3. Reduce recordable manual-handling incidents by at least 20%.
4. Maintain customer service during implementation.
5. Avoid irreversible supplier dependency where a viable staged design exists.

Targeted conveyor maintenance, scanner replacement, and temporary seasonal labour.
