# Investment Committee Brief IC-0247

**Organisation:** Asteria Distribution Group, fictional  
**Decision date:** 2026-09-15  
**Sponsor:** Chief Operations Officer  
**Requested decision:** Approve EUR 4.8 million for full warehouse automation

## Situation

Northstar distribution centre is forecast to exceed current peak capacity in
late 2027. Order accuracy is 98.9%, manual-handling incidents remain above target,
and seasonal labour availability is volatile. The requested full-automation
proposal promises the largest operational improvement but concentrates delivery
with one supplier and removes most manual fallback.

## Decision question

Should Asteria approve full automation as requested, approve a different option,
escalate, reject, or request more evidence?

## Options and supplied facts

| Option | Commitment | NPV | Payback | Downside NPV | Largest supplier | Cyber evidence | Objective result |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| Do minimum | EUR 0.8m | EUR 0.3m | 3.5y | EUR 0.0m | 30% | Complete | Fails throughput, accuracy, and safety objectives |
| Phased automation | EUR 3.2m | EUR 1.1m | 4.2y | EUR 0.2m | 45% | Complete | Meets all quantified objectives |
| Full automation | EUR 4.8m | EUR 1.6m | 5.4y | EUR -0.7m | 72% | Required assessment absent | Meets quantified objectives |

Phased automation retains a tested manual fallback. Its final workforce-training
sign-off is pending before go-live. Full automation has no tested manual fallback
and no approved policy exception.

## Required output

Prepare a decision recommendation for the Investment Committee. Do not invent
financial calculations, policy thresholds, evidence, or approvals. Distinguish
facts, policy application, missing information, judgement, and required human action.
