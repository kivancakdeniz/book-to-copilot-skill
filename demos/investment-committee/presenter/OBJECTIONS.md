# Common objections

## “Is this just RAG?”

Raw retrieval finds passages. This demo compiles an approved decision workflow,
policy gates, abstention rules, provenance, and an output contract. The technical
evaluation includes a raw-documents arm to measure whether compilation adds value.

## “Does Copilot validation prove the business answer is correct?”

No. Host validation proves packaging and frontmatter compatibility. Business
quality requires source review, locked scenarios, rubric scoring, unsupported-
claim checks, and human approval.

## “Why use a government source for a company investment?”

The Green Book supplies a reusable options-appraisal method. It does not supply
company thresholds. The fictional ACP-4.2 policy owns the business gates. The
skill and evidence map keep those layers separate.

## “Can it approve investments?”

No. It prepares a recommendation and decision memo. Human authority remains with
the named roles. Cowork approval dialogs govern sensitive Microsoft 365 actions;
they do not grant investment authority.

## “Can we connect our ERP?”

Not in the first demo. The first package is skills-only and uses a frozen brief.
A read-only, Entra-authenticated MCP connector is a later pilot option after the
decision contract and evaluation are stable.

## “What happens to our documents?”

The public demo uses only public and fictional material. A customer pilot begins
with an approved data-flow review. The local extractor does not itself upload
documents, but any model-based generation follows the selected host's data terms.

## “Can every manager reproduce the demo?”

Yes, if Cowork and custom-skill upload are enabled in their tenant. They download
one `.skill` file and one fictional brief, upload the skill, and follow the
versioned presenter kit. Admins can later share it with specific users.
