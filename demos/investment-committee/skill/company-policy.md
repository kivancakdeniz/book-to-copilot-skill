# Company Policy

## Status

This is a compact rendering of **Asteria Capital Allocation Policy ACP-4.2**, effective 2026-01-01. Asteria Distribution Group and all policy content are fictional and synthetic. These rules are the company decision thresholds; they are not Green Book requirements.

## Decision classes

- **Approve:** mandatory evidence complete, all mandatory gates pass, and named authority sufficient.
- **Conditional approval:** strategic, financial, downside, concentration, and cyber gates pass; only remediable implementation conditions remain, each with an owner and due date before commitment or go-live.
- **Escalate:** higher authority, explicit strategic exception, severe supplier concentration, or unresolved conflict requires a higher body.
- **Reject:** mandatory objective or financial/downside gate fails without an approved strategic exception, or an unmitigable critical risk remains.
- **Insufficient evidence:** a required fact is absent or contradictory, so no defensible class can be assigned.

## Mandatory rules

| Rule | Requirement | Consequence when unmet |
|---|---|---|
| **ACP-F01** | Risk-adjusted NPV must be greater than EUR 0. | Reject unless a documented strategic exception is escalated. |
| **ACP-F02** | Base-case payback must be 5.0 years or less. | Escalate a documented exception; otherwise reject the option. |
| **ACP-F03** | Downside-case NPV must be zero or positive. | Reject unless a documented strategic exception is escalated. |
| **ACP-E01** | Base, downside, and sensitivity evidence must be present and internally consistent. | Insufficient evidence. |
| **ACP-O01** | Compare at least do-minimum, phased, and requested options against the same objectives. | Insufficient evidence. |
| **ACP-S01** | No supplier may receive more than 50% of implementation spend without a mitigation plan. | 51-65%: conditional approval; above 65%: escalate. |
| **ACP-C01** | A documented operational-technology cyber assessment is mandatory. | Missing assessment: insufficient evidence. Known pending remediations: conditional approval only, with CISO owner and due date before contract award. |
| **ACP-R01** | Identify workforce, training, cutover, continuity, and benefit-owner plans. | Remediable gaps may be conditions; missing ownership is insufficient evidence. |
| **ACP-M01** | Benefits require named owners, measures, baselines, targets, review dates, and stop/review conditions. | Conditional approval until completed. |

## Authority matrix

| Total commitment | Minimum human authority |
|---|---|
| Up to EUR 1.0 million | Business-unit EVP and Finance Director |
| Above EUR 1.0 million through EUR 5.0 million | Investment Committee: CFO, COO, and CIO |
| Above EUR 5.0 million | Board approval after Investment Committee recommendation |

Additional participation:

- CISO joins when ACP-C01 is pending or failed.
- Procurement joins when ACP-S01 is above 50%.
- Legal joins when non-standard liability, data, or service terms are requested.

## Judgement and human boundary

NPV alone does not determine the decision. Consider objectives, monetised and non-monetised effects, operational distribution, delivery feasibility, risk and uncertainty, and monitoring credibility. Quantitative outputs must not conceal missing evidence.

Copilot may prepare a recommendation, identify rules, and draft a memo. It cannot approve investment, release budget, select a supplier, sign a contract, or invent missing financial calculations.
