# Claim Review Method

This reference compiles concise review methods from three US Federal Trade
Commission publications. It does not quote long passages, provide legal advice,
state a final legal conclusion, or set Lumena policy.

## Method rules

1. Identify the advertisement's overall express and implied messages from the
   full presentation, not only isolated words.
2. For objective claims, identify the evidence available before dissemination
   and test whether it addresses the claim actually conveyed.
3. Keep qualifications understandable and prominent enough to be noticed with
   the claim they qualify. A disclosure should not contradict the main message.
4. In digital journeys, consider proximity, prominence, presentation, device and
   screen constraints, scrolling, hyperlinks, repetition, and whether a user can
   avoid the disclosure before acting.
5. Match disclosures to the relevant modality. Audio or video claims may require
   disclosures in the content, not solely distant surrounding text.
6. Identify material connections between an endorser and advertiser. Make the
   connection clear where people encounter each endorsement, and do not assume a
   platform label or ambiguous shorthand communicates it adequately.
7. Do not let an endorsement communicate a performance claim broader than the
   advertiser's evidence supports.

These are method prompts for identifying review issues. Apply the fictional
MCS-2.1 rules for demo decisions and route legal questions to human Legal review.
