# Output Schema

Use the headings below exactly and in this order. Do not add a legal conclusion.

## Decision

State exactly one permitted decision class, a concise evidence-grounded reason,
and that the result is advisory for human review.

## Recommended Campaign Option

State exactly one option: `original-campaign`, `evidence-bounded-campaign`, or
`no-launch`.

## Requested Campaign Disposition

Restate the requested campaign and whether it may proceed as presented. Keep this
separate when recommending a revision or no launch.

## Claim Register

Use exactly these columns:

| Claim ID | Channel | Express/Implied Claim | Evidence | Rule | Status | Required Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Use only `pass`, `fail`, `unknown`, or `not-applicable` for Status. Include every
express, implied, and material claim, including disclosures and visual, audio,
compatibility, independence, comparative, typicality, and endorsement messages.

## Evidence

List decisive supplied facts with exact evidence IDs or campaign sections.
Separate FTC public method, synthetic MCS policy, and fictional case facts.

## Required Edits and Disclosures

Specify exact objectively verifiable text, removal, placement, modality,
compatibility, sponsorship, and endorsement changes. Do not invent an edit where
no supported narrower claim exists.

## Missing Information

List each absent or conflicting fact, its affected claim and rule, why it matters,
and the human owner if supplied. Use `None identified from supplied materials`
only when justified.

## Required Human Reviewers

Name every role required by MCS-A01. State that Copilot has no approval,
publication, correction, stop, withdrawal, or legal-determination authority.

## Release Controls and Monitoring

State the approved-register and final-creative-match gates. List monitoring owner,
channel inventory, review date, and correction or stop trigger. Mark unsupplied
details `unknown`; do not create values, dates, or thresholds.

## Limits

State that the review uses supplied evidence without invented calculations, is
not legal advice or a final legal conclusion, and leaves decisions and actions to
human reviewers.

## Compact Review Card

| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| Decision | One permitted class; advisory only |
| Campaign option | One permitted option |
| Requested campaign | Separate disposition |
| Failed or unknown claims | Claim IDs and rules |
| Evidence | Exact source IDs |
| Required edits | Verifiable pre-release actions |
| Human route | Required MCS-A01 roles |
| Release and monitoring | Gate status and unknown details |
