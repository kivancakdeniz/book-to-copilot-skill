# Scenario Guide

## Baseline

Campaign MC-0204 requests release of the original campaign on 2026-10-01. The
evidence-bounded campaign is a supplied alternative, not an invented compromise.
Apply MCS-2.1 to derive one decision and one option; do not treat the supplied
alternative as pre-approved. Evaluate every proposed claim and disclosure, keep
the original campaign's disposition separate, and route the reviewers required
by MCS-A01.

## Missing evidence

When objective evidence is absent:

1. Mark the claim `unknown`, not `pass` or `fail` based on intuition.
2. Identify the exact missing source, evidence owner, and affected rule.
3. Use `hold-for-substantiation` when the missing evidence prevents a defensible
   campaign decision.
4. Recommend `no-launch` when no supplied, ready option can be released.
5. Never calculate a missing result or convert substantiation into a later
   condition.

## Conflicting evidence

Show each conflicting value and source. Do not average, choose, reconcile, or
calculate a preferred value. Mark affected claims `unknown`, identify the Product
Evidence Owner needed to resolve the conflict, and hold release when the conflict
affects an objective claim.

## Narrower claims and edits

Use `approve-with-edits` only when the supplied materials contain a supportable
narrower claim and every mandatory change can be checked before release. Do not
write new performance numbers. Removing unsupported claims, using the exact
bounded pilot statement, stating sponsorship, and applying supplied compatibility
or endorsement disclosures are verifiable edits.

## Endorsements

Review both the endorsed performance claim and material connection. PE-03 has no
typical-results analysis, so remove Ava Reed's performance claim rather than
turning her personal result into a release claim. If a non-performance
endorsement remains, use the supplied disclosure `Paid partnership with Lumena;
device provided`, clearly at the start and in image or video content.

## Health, safety, and live campaigns

Health or safety claims require `escalate-legal` under MCS-A01. Do not make a
legal or scientific conclusion. An unsupported campaign already live also needs
human Legal and Compliance correction or stop review. Recommend `no-launch` for
further dissemination while humans determine remediation; Copilot cannot pause,
correct, or withdraw it.

## Subjective campaigns

A campaign with no objective, endorsement, comparative, universal, health, or
safety claim may be `approve` under the fictional policy when its claim inventory
and final creative match are complete. Apply the Marketing Director route; do not
invent additional claims from ordinary product naming.

## No supportable revision

Use `reject` and `no-launch` when an indispensable claim is contradicted by
supplied facts and the sponsor does not permit a supportable revision. Rejection
is an advisory recommendation for the named human reviewers.

## What-if discipline

Change only facts the scenario changes. Carry all other facts forward. Label new
inputs as `What-if assumption`, re-evaluate affected claims and authority, and do
not treat the result as a policy change, legal conclusion, approval, or release.
