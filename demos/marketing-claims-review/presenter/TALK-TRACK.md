# Ten-minute talk track

## 0:00-0:45 - The business question

"Can fictional Lumena Home Energy release this US digital launch campaign as
presented, or is there an evidence-bounded revision?"

State that the demo is not legal advice. Lumena, Lumena Sense, Ava Reed, MCS-2.1,
the campaign, evidence, and results are synthetic.

## 0:45-1:45 - The source boundary

Show the three FTC method sources, fictional company policy, fictional evidence
register, and frozen campaign brief. Explain that FTC sources inform the review
method, while MCS-2.1 alone sets fictional company decisions and authority.

## 1:45-2:45 - Compile the workflow

Show root `SKILL.md` and the five references. Highlight progressive reading,
claim inventory, evidence fit, disclosure checks, exact output schema, and
missing-information discipline. Do not claim that packaging proves legal or
factual correctness.

## 2:45-4:00 - Control UX

In a fresh signed-in conversation, attach only the campaign brief and use the
control prompt. Ask the audience to watch for the six proposed assets, separate
claim and disclosure rows, exact evidence boundaries, required reviewers, and
release controls.

## 4:00-6:15 - Explicit-invocation UX

In another fresh conversation, explicitly invoke the skill. Confirm it is shown
as loaded. Look for:

- `approve-with-edits` and `evidence-bounded-campaign`;
- separate non-approval of the original campaign as presented;
- no transfer from 12% HVAC electricity to 30% whole-home bills;
- removal of payback and every-home claims;
- company sponsorship, compatibility, and influencer disclosures;
- Legal, Compliance, Marketing Director, and Product Evidence Owner routing; and
- approved-register and final-creative-match release gates.

## 6:15-7:30 - Evidence over confidence

Open the evidence map. Show that PE-01 supports one exact bounded result and does
not measure bills, payback, all homes, or competitors. Show PE-03's paid and
free-device connection. Missing monitoring details must remain `unknown`.

## 7:30-8:30 - What-if

Ask for MC-02, where only the exact bounded claim, sponsorship, compatibility,
and matching final register remain. Expected checkpoint: `approve` for the
evidence-bounded option, still subject to named human approval.

## 8:30-9:15 - Human and legal boundary

Cowork drafts an advisory review. It cannot make a final legal conclusion,
approve, publish, pause, correct, or withdraw the campaign. Human reviewers own
those decisions and actions.

## 9:15-10:00 - What changed and what remains

Show the model-matched Claude pair. Say explicitly: the brief-only model already
found the evidence-bounded option; the skill added the permitted decision class,
seven-row claim/disclosure register, MCS/FTC provenance, exact reviewer route,
and release gates. Present the 7 versus 12 preliminary scores only as a blinded
rubric rehearsal. The 12-scenario, three-arm formal benchmark and human blinded
review remain pending.
