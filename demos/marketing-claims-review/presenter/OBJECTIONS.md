# Objections and responses

## "Is this legal advice?"

No. The skill prepares an advisory claim review against supplied evidence and
fictional MCS-2.1 policy. It does not make a final legal conclusion. Human Legal
and Compliance reviewers retain their roles.

## "Are these real customer or product results?"

No. Lumena Home Energy, Lumena Sense, Ava Reed, the policy, campaign, pilot,
compatibility facts, payments, and every result are fictional synthetic data.

## "Does the FTC material automatically determine the answer?"

No. The FTC publications supply concise public review methods. MCS-2.1 supplies
the fictional decision classes, release control, and authority route. The skill
must keep those provenance layers separate.

## "Does a 12% pilot result justify a broad savings headline?"

No. The supplied evidence is limited to median HVAC electricity reduction in a
specific self-selected pilot population, duration, baseline, and eligibility
conditions. It did not measure whole-home bills, payback, all homes, competitors,
or typical influencer results.

## "Why not let the model write a better claim?"

It may use only a supported narrower claim already supplied by the source pack.
It cannot invent performance language, calculate an outcome, or fill an evidence
gap. Where no supportable revision exists, it must hold, escalate, or reject.

## "Does the skill stop a live campaign?"

No. It can identify an escalation and recommend human correction or stop review.
Copilot cannot approve, publish, pause, correct, or withdraw campaign content.

## "Does the control-versus-treatment demo prove the skill works?"

No. It illustrates signed-in Cowork UX under two prompt conditions. The host may
not expose a pinned model or deterministic skill discovery. Formal evidence
requires the separate locked 12-scenario benchmark, declared runtime controls,
randomized arms, and blinded review.

## "Can we use a real campaign next?"

Only in an approved private pilot with authorized source material, privacy and
security review, recorded provenance, and retained Legal and Compliance review.
The public demo must remain synthetic.
