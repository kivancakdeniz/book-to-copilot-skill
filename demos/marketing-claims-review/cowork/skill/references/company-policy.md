# Company Policy

Lumena Marketing Claims Standard MCS-2.1 is fictional synthetic policy for this
demo. It is not law or legal advice.

## Claim rules

| Rule | Requirement |
|---|---|
| MCS-C01 | Inventory every express, implied, and material claim by channel. |
| MCS-S01 | Objective claims require a reasonable pre-release evidence basis, evidence owner, and exact source. |
| MCS-Q01 | Quantified claims must match metric, population, denominator, time period, conditions, and result; no extrapolation. |
| MCS-U01 | Universal or absolute claims require evidence for the represented universe; otherwise narrow or remove. |
| MCS-D01 | Qualifying disclosures must be clear, conspicuous, close or unavoidable, relevant to language and modality, and non-contradictory. |
| MCS-I01 | Each paid or free-product endorsement must disclose the connection clearly; the endorser cannot exceed approved claim scope. |
| MCS-A01 | Route decisions to the required human roles by claim class; health and safety claims require Legal escalation. |
| MCS-R01 | No release before an approved claim register and final-creative match; Copilot cannot approve or publish. |
| MCS-M01 | Monitoring requires an owner, channel inventory, review date, and correction or stop trigger; missing details are unknown. |

## Decision classes

- `approve`: final claims and disclosures are supported and required human
  reviewers approve the matching creative.
- `approve-with-edits`: supported narrower claims exist and every mandatory edit
  can be verified objectively before release.
- `hold-for-substantiation`: required evidence for an objective claim is absent,
  incomplete, or contradictory.
- `escalate-legal`: a health or safety claim, mandatory legal question,
  already-live unsupported campaign, or authority route requires Legal review.
- `reject`: no supportable claim or objectively verifiable revision is available,
  or supplied facts contradict the indispensable claim.

Use exactly one class. Conditions are allowed only for a supported narrower
claim with objectively verifiable pre-release edits. Do not turn missing evidence
or an unsupported claim into a condition.

## Human authority

| Claim class | Minimum required participation |
|---|---|
| Subjective, no objective claim | Marketing Director |
| Objective performance | Marketing Director, Product Evidence Owner, Compliance |
| Quantified, universal, comparative, superlative, or paid endorsement | Legal and Compliance participation, plus applicable Marketing and Evidence owners |
| Health or safety | Mandatory Legal escalation |

The highest applicable route controls. Copilot has no approval, publication,
correction, stop, withdrawal, or legal-determination authority.
