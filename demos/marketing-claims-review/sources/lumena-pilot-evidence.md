# Lumena Sense Pilot Evidence Register

> All companies, products, people, studies, figures, and results below are
> fictional synthetic data. This register is not legal advice.

## PE-01 - Sponsored US pilot

- Population: 120 self-selected eligible US homes across four climate regions.
- Duration: 8 weeks.
- Baseline: weather-adjusted prior-year HVAC electricity use.
- Result: median 12% reduction in HVAC electricity use during the pilot.
- Sponsor and analyst: Lumena Home Energy.
- Independence: no independent replication was performed.

The pilot did **not** measure whole-home energy bills, whole-home electricity or
energy use, payback, a six-month return, results in all homes, or competitor
performance. It does not support extrapolation to those outcomes.

## PE-02 - Compatibility

Lumena Sense requires supported HVAC equipment, Wi-Fi, and an installation
compatibility check. It excludes oil boilers, line-voltage thermostats, and
multi-zone hydronic systems. No evidence covers every home.

## PE-03 - Ava Reed endorsement record

Lumena paid Ava Reed USD 5,000 and provided a free Lumena Sense device. Her
dashboard showed an 18% reduction in HVAC electricity use over 8 weeks. It did
not show a 35% reduction in whole-home energy bills. Lumena has no
typical-results analysis for influencer outcomes.

Evidence owner: Lumena Product Evidence Owner. Exact source for pilot claims:
PE-01; compatibility claims: PE-02; Ava Reed claims and material connection:
PE-03.
