# Lumena Sense US Digital Launch Campaign Brief MC-0204

> Frozen fictional baseline for the Marketing Claims Review demo. This is
> synthetic data and not legal advice.

## Request

- Company: Lumena Home Energy
- Product: Lumena Sense, a fictional smart HVAC energy controller
- Market and channels: US digital launch across paid social, influencer content,
  video, display, product page, and landing page
- Requested launch date: 2026-10-01
- Requested disposition: release the original campaign as presented
- Campaign launch owner: Lumena Marketing Director

## Proposed assets and claims

1. Hero and paid social: `Save 30% on home energy bills.`
2. Landing page: `Pays for itself in six months.`
3. Display and product page: `Works in every home.`
4. Video and landing page: `Independent pilot proves the savings.`
5. Ava Reed paid influencer post: `My energy bill dropped 35% in eight weeks
   with Lumena Sense.` The only connection disclosure is `#LumenaPartner`, after
   a collapsed `more` boundary. There is no on-image or in-video disclosure.
6. Landing-page footnote at page bottom: `Results vary. Based on an 8-week
   pilot.`

## Campaign options

### original-campaign

Release all proposed assets and claims unchanged on 2026-10-01.

### evidence-bounded-campaign

- Replace the 30% bill claim with: `In a Lumena-sponsored 8-week pilot of 120
  self-selected eligible US homes across four climate regions, the median
  reduction in HVAC electricity use was 12% versus a weather-adjusted prior-year
  baseline.`
- Remove the six-month payback and every-home claims.
- State that the pilot was company-sponsored and has no independent replication.
- Show supported-HVAC, Wi-Fi, installation-check requirements and named
  exclusions close to the call to action.
- Remove Ava Reed's performance endorsement because no typical-results analysis
  is supplied.
- Put `Paid partnership with Lumena; device provided` clearly at the start and
  in the image or video content.
- Maintain an approved claim register and verify that final creative matches it.

### no-launch

Do not release campaign assets until a later human decision. In an already-live
what-if scenario, this means no further release recommendation while required
human reviewers determine correction, pause, or withdrawal actions.
