# Responsible use

`book-to-copilot-skill` and its demos are tools for extraction, structured
review, and evaluation. They are not legal, medical, engineering, financial, or
other professional advice. All included organizations, policies, cases, people,
and evidence are synthetic unless a source is explicitly identified as public.

## Human authority

AI output may organize evidence and propose a review path, but an authorized
human remains responsible for interpretation, approval, and action. Do not use a
demo or generated skill to autonomously:

- file a regulatory, legal, tax, safety, or compliance submission;
- send messages or notices to customers, regulators, employees, or the public;
- onboard, reject, suspend, or close a customer, account, transaction, product,
  campaign, facility, or service;
- publish claims, guidance, policies, medical content, or engineering decisions;
- stop production, work, access, payment, treatment, or a safety process.

## Sources and data

Verify source freshness, jurisdiction, version, effective date, amendments, and
superseding guidance at the official URL before relying on a result. A short
method summary is not the official text.

Do not place secrets, credentials, tenant identifiers, customer data, personal
data, confidential documents, or regulated records in demos, screenshots,
prompts, model traces, issue reports, or published evidence. Use approved,
minimum-necessary, synthetic or properly de-identified data. Follow the model
provider's data terms and the organization's retention, residency, access,
deletion, and incident-response controls.

## Regulated deployment review

Before production or regulated use, require review by the relevant legal,
privacy, security, compliance, medical, engineering, risk, records-management,
and business owners. Validate permissions, model/provider terms, human approval
gates, logging, monitoring, rollback, accessibility, bias and error handling,
source updates, and an alternative process when the system is unavailable.

## Evaluation limitations

Repository evaluations use small, synthetic, frozen scenarios and selected
models or host versions. They do not establish production accuracy, legal
compliance, safety, fairness, robustness, generalization, or future performance.
Model and host updates can change results. Screenshots are point-in-time evidence
and may omit intermediate steps. Scores should be reproduced and reviewed rather
than treated as certification.

## Screenshots, marks, and model output

Screenshots may show third-party UI and trademarks only to document evaluation
context. Their owners do not sponsor or endorse this project. AI model outputs
can be inaccurate, incomplete, or contain unsupported material and must be
reviewed before they are used or published. See
[THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

## Incidents

If a result could cause harm, disclosure, an unauthorized action, or a regulated
decision, stop the affected workflow, preserve only the minimum safe audit
record, notify the responsible human owner, follow the organization's incident
process, and do not publish sensitive details. Report repository security issues
through the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
